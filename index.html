About the Event

Where Princeton Ingenuity Meets Claude AI

Anthropic and Princeton University are partnering to host the most exciting AI hackathon of the year. Whether you're a first-time hacker or a seasoned engineer, this is your chance to build something remarkable.

Claude API Access

Every team gets generous API credits to build with Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 3 Haiku, and access to the latest Claude models. Build agents, assistants, tools — anything you can imagine.

Mentorship

Anthropic engineers and researchers will be on-site to help you navigate the Claude API, brainstorm ideas, and debug your projects in real time.

Community

Connect with 300+ hackers from Princeton and top universities across the country. Build your network, form teams, and make lasting friendships.

Princeton Campus

Hack from the stunning Princeton campus — food, drinks, and sleeping space provided. Experience the historic Gothic architecture while building the future.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate?

All currently enrolled college students are welcome. Princeton students get priority registration, but we welcome students from any university.

Do I need a team?

Teams of 1–4 people. Don't have a team? Come to the mixer on Friday night — we'll help you find teammates with complementary skills.

Do I need AI experience?

Not at all! We'll have workshops and Anthropic engineers on-site to help you get started with the Claude API from scratch.

What should I build with?

Projects must use the Claude API in a meaningful way. Beyond that, use any languages, frameworks, or tools you like.

Is travel reimbursed?

We offer limited travel grants for students traveling from other universities. Apply in your registration and we'll review on a case-by-case basis.

What about food and sleep?

All meals, snacks, and drinks are provided throughout the event. Sleeping space is available, and there are dorms nearby for Princeton students.
